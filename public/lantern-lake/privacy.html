Lantern Lake

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Summary: Lantern Lake is designed as an offline single-player game. The current release does not use accounts, ads, analytics, crash-reporting SDKs, in-app purchases, Firebase, or a developer-operated backend.

Data collection and sharing

Lantern Lake does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal or sensitive user data with the developer or third parties through the game's runtime.

The game does not request or use location, contacts, microphone, camera, advertising ID, SMS, call logs, health data, or files/media for gameplay.

Local data

Lantern Lake stores gameplay progress and preferences locally on the device. This can include completed voyages and best results, discovered routes, Lantern Points, purchased upgrades, onboarding completion, and audio/control/accessibility/quality settings.

This data is used only to provide game functionality and is not transmitted to the developer. Android cloud backup is disabled for the production build.

Deleting local data

Players can remove local game data from Settings → Reset Local Data. Local data can also be removed by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the game.

Device features

Touch input, audio playback, and optional vibration/haptics are processed locally on the device. The Android release may declare the VIBRATE permission solely for haptic feedback.

Google Play

Google Play may independently process information when a user searches for, downloads, installs, updates, reviews, or otherwise interacts with the game through Google Play. That processing is controlled by Google and subject to Google's terms and privacy policies.

Changes

If a future release adds analytics, advertising, accounts, online services, purchases, or another feature that changes data handling, this policy and the Google Play Data Safety declaration will be updated before that release is distributed.
